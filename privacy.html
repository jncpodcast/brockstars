YOUR PRIVACY.

The Brock Stars is operated by Brad Brock Music. We use your email to send secure sign-in links and identify your account. Your profile, points, show check-ins, trivia answers, Bingo progress, requests venue perk redemptions and Side Quest completion claims are stored online so they can work across devices.

Side Quest completion notes and photo links are visible to Brad for review. Do not include someone else’s private contact details. Sharing a photo does not automatically grant permission to repost it.

Your display name and show scores may appear to other signed-in fans. Your email is available to Brad for account administration, including a notification when you first join. A shoutout or dedication may be spoken aloud during the show.

Show-news emails are optional. Change that preference in My account. Signing up does not automatically opt you into marketing.

The service uses GitHub Pages for the website, Cloudflare for account and show data, and Resend for email delivery. Your browser stores a sign-in token and temporary pending actions so you can stay signed in and retry a connection failure.

To request correction or deletion of your account, email brockentertainment@gmail.com.
